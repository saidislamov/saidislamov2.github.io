Kyrgyz Manti recipe

Kyrgyz manti has a rich background that is likely rooted in Central Asian nomadic cultures, with possible origins among the Turkic-speaking Uighurs in northwestern China, and its spread along the Silk Road. Nomadic peoples, including the ancient Turkic and Mongol horsemen, carried manti with them as a convenient, quick meal that could be steamed or boiled over a campfire. Today, it is a beloved traditional dish in Kyrgyzstan, often featuring a filling of ground meat (typically lamb or beef), onions, and sometimes potatoes or pumpkin.

Ingredients

For the dough:

  • 3 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup of warm water
  • 1 teaspoon of salt (adjust to your own likings)
  • 1 tablespoon of vegetable oil (optional)

For the filling:

  1. 1 lb of ground beef
  2. 2-3 onions, finely chopped
  3. 1 medium potato,finely diced (optional but traditional)
  4. 1 teaspoon of salt(adjust to taste)
  5. 1 teaspoon of black pepper
  6. 2-3 tablespoons of melted butter

Preperation

  1. Divide the dough into several smaller balls.
  2. Roll them into thin sheets.
  3. Place a spoonful of filling in the center.
  4. Fold them up, to form a traditional manti shape.

For steaming:

  • Apply butter to the sides of the steamer, so it does not stick.
  • Place them with a little space in between.
  • Steam for 40-45 minutes until fully cooked.
